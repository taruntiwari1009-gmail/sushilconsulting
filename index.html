Data, decoded · A practical mini lesson

One number rarely tells
the whole story.

Data becomes useful when you connect the numbers, ask the right question and understand what they mean for a decision. Let's look at a simple business example.

LESSON 01 / REVENUE VS PROFIT

Which business is actually doing better?

Both businesses sell similar products. Business A generates more revenue. Business B keeps a larger share of every rupee it earns.

RevenueThe money earned from sales before expenses.
ProfitWhat remains after costs and expenses are paid.
Profit marginThe percentage of revenue that becomes profit.

THE BUSINESS INSIGHTGrowth and profitability are not the same thing.

If you only track revenue, you might celebrate the wrong result. Looking at revenue alongside costs and margins gives leaders a more complete picture before they decide where to invest, increase prices or reduce waste.
